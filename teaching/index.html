Teaching Assistant — University of Huddersfield

Role: Teaching Assistant, School of Computing and Engineering — I deliver the hands-on lab sessions for each module. Period: January 2023 to present. Audience: Postgraduate MSc students.

Modules

Large Databases (MSc) Hands-on lab sessions covering relational database design, query optimisation, and large-scale data management. Topics include indexing strategies, transaction management, and working with production-scale datasets.

→ Module outline (PDF): twelve one-hour lectures and twelve two-hour labs (36 contact hours), from the relational model to distributed, large-scale systems.

Data Mining (MSc) Hands-on lab sessions covering supervised and unsupervised learning techniques applied to real-world datasets: classification, clustering, association-rule mining, and evaluation methodology.

→ Module outline (PDF): twelve one-hour lectures and twelve two-hour labs (36 contact hours), from statistical foundations to honest evaluation and scale.

Other contributions

  • Student mentoring and curriculum development, contributing alongside the module leaders.
  • An optional data-analysis workshop for less confident MSc students, outside the timetabled labs.
  • Examination invigilation for postgraduate and undergraduate students.
