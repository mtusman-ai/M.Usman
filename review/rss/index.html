How to follow Signal to Decision in a feed reader: the feed address to copy, and what the feed carries.

Signal to Decision has an RSS feed. A feed reader checks it for you and shows each new issue when it goes up on this site, without email.

The feed address: https://mtusman-ai.github.io/M.Usman/review/feed.xml

To follow it, copy that address and add it in your feed reader, usually under "Add feed" or "Subscribe". Most readers also find the feed if you give them the address of this site.

The feed carries every issue on this site: its title, a one-line summary, the date it went up and a link to the full issue. Opened in a browser, the feed address shows the feed's raw code. That is normal, and the address still works in a feed reader.
