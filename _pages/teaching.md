## Teaching Assistant — University of Huddersfield

**Role:** Teaching Assistant, School of Computing and Engineering.
**Period:** September 2022 – present.
**Audience:** Postgraduate MSc students.

### Modules

**Large Databases (MSc)**
Hands-on labs covering relational database design, query optimisation, and large-scale data management. Topics include indexing strategies, transaction management, and working with production-scale datasets.

**Data Mining (MSc)**
Labs and lectures covering supervised and unsupervised learning techniques applied to real-world datasets: classification, clustering, association-rule mining, and evaluation methodology.

### Other contributions

- MSc dissertation mentoring — advising students on research methodology, experimental design, and technical writing.
- Curriculum development input alongside module leaders.
