Signal to Decision is monthly decision intelligence for AI transformation, read through the lens of the Insights profession. It is written for the analysts, architects, engineers and scientists who turn enterprise data and production AI into decisions. Each edition asks what actually matters for the business, not what trended. It reviews a complete calendar month and publishes in the first week of the next, so every claim is reviewed in arrears against a closed period. The launch edition is the exception: it consolidates the year to 6 June 2026, to set the context and the standard before the regular monthly rhythm begins.

### Latest edition: Signal to Decision — June 2026

June 2026 moved the enterprise AI question from which model to which posture: what you build, what you buy, and what you can walk away from when the price, the licence or a government changes the terms. An agentic interaction now costs about thirty times a 2023 chatbot exchange on EY's analysis, a US export order kept two frontier models offline worldwide for eighteen days, and the open-weight tier reached claimed parity at a fraction of the price, on the makers' own benchmarks.

New from this edition: **the month, chair by chair** — the same June read from every seat at the leadership table, one row per chair, with the question to take to your next agenda.

**The lead read:** make, buy, or boost, one decision per workload; you can only route what you have governed.
