19:T10f6,<h2 id="who-is-responsible">Who is responsible</h2>
<p>The Signal to Decision email list is run by M Usman, as an individual, in the UK. I decide what happens to the details you give when you sign up. To contact me about them, reply to any email from the list, or send me a message on <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/tariqusman/" rel="noopener">LinkedIn</a>.</p>
<h2 id="what-i-collect">What I collect</h2>
<ul>
<li>Your email address, to send you the briefing.</li>
<li>Your full name, to address you.</li>
<li>Your profession or role, your country and your town, to understand who reads the briefing and which decisions to cover.</li>
<li>Anything you choose to add in the optional box.</li>
<li>A record of your consent: the words you agreed to, their version and the date.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="why-and-on-what-basis">Why, and on what basis</h2>
<p>I use your details only to send you Signal to Decision, about once a month, and to understand my readers as a group. The lawful basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time through the unsubscribe link in every email. Withdrawing does not affect anything sent before. I do not sell or share your details, and I make no automated decisions about you.</p>
<h2 id="confirming-your-address">Confirming your address</h2>
<p>After you sign up, you receive an email with a confirmation link. You join the list only when you confirm. If you neither confirm nor unsubscribe within 30 days of your last sign-up, I delete your details, as set out below.</p>
<h2 id="where-your-details-are-kept">Where your details are kept</h2>
<ul>
<li>The sign-up form is a Google Form. When you open it, Google's own privacy policy applies to that page.</li>
<li>Your details are kept in a private Google Sheet in my Google account. Confirmation emails are sent from that account.</li>
<li>A copy is kept in my private notes, stored in Microsoft OneDrive.</li>
</ul>
<p>Google and Microsoft store this data for me, and their servers may be outside the UK. This website itself sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts.</p>
<h2 id="how-long-i-keep-them">How long I keep them</h2>
<ul>
<li>Sign-ups that are never confirmed or unsubscribed: deleted 30 days after your last sign-up. Each new sign-up before you confirm starts the 30 days again.</li>
<li>While you are subscribed: kept until you unsubscribe.</li>
<li>After you unsubscribe, even before confirming: your name, role, country, town and message are deleted after 30 days. The rest of your record is kept as proof that you withdrew consent. It holds your email address, the words you agreed to and their version, and the date of each step from sign-up to leaving. It also holds the number of confirmation emails sent, the code in your email links, reference numbers, and a note of the last change with its date.</li>
<li>Each raw form response, including one that does not pass my checks: deleted 30 days after it arrives.</li>
</ul>
<p>These deletions run once a day, so each can take up to about a day longer.</p>
<p>Google Sheets and OneDrive keep earlier versions of a file, and OneDrive keeps deleted notes in its recycle bin. So after a scheduled deletion, earlier copies may remain in those services for a time. My mailbox may also keep copies of the emails sent to you. I do not use any of these copies.</p>
<h2 id="your-rights">Your rights</h2>
<p>You can ask me for a copy of your details, or ask me to correct or delete them. You can also ask me to restrict or stop their use, or to send them to you in a portable form. To ask, contact me as above. I reply within one month.</p>
<p>If you ask me to delete your details, I delete them from the sheet and my notes. I delete every form response that carries your email address, even one that did not pass my checks. I clear the sheet's version history and remove your deleted note from the OneDrive recycle bin. In my mailbox, I delete the emails sent to you and any notice that one could not be delivered. Then I delete them forever from the mailbox's bin.</p>
<p>You can also complain to the <a href="https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/" rel="noopener">Information Commissioner's Office</a>.</p>
