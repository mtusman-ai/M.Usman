    End-to-end NLP service: a sentiment classifier extended with a retrieval-augmented-generation (RAG) layer, served behind a containerised FastAPI API with CI. Served baselines 0.768 (Naïve Bayes) and 0.834 (VADER) on the public NLTK twitter_samples set.

    Direct multi-horizon forecasting on the public ETTh1 benchmark, served behind an API with CI; an honest back-test shows gradient boosting leading at short horizons (1h, 6h) and persistence and seasonal baselines stronger from 12h out.

    Reproducible R/tidymodels mortality prediction on the UCI Heart Failure Clinical Records set; admission-only random forest ROC AUC 0.730, with explicit leakage handling.

    The scikit-learn companion and a Data Mining teaching example; admission-only random forest ROC AUC 0.817 (10-fold cross-validation mean 0.771).
